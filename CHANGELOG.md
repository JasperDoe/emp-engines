# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (in progress)

- Repo set up: packwiz pack, server layer, build and test tools.
- Mod list drafted in `mods.txt`; not yet boot-tested.
- Quest book (FTB Quests), guide and tutorial only: Briefing, Gears & Metals, March of Industry 1-5, Into the Unknown 1, 2, 2.5, Logistics. Age chapters open when the age is researched. Generated by `tools/questgen/build_quests.py`.
- Research locks (Chapters): andesite, alloy, brass, electricity, exotic, flight, logistics. Anything crafted from a locked item is locked too (`tools/questgen/build_stages.py`). Crafting is locked and recipes are hidden in JEI until researched; items can still be held and traded.
- Nether 1:1 override removed (Amplified Nether decides the Nether layout). Nether and End locked behind server-wide unlocks (`/skybound unlock nether|end`).
- Shaders on by default: Iris + Complementary Reimagined, tuned light (low shadows, low light shafts), soft distant fog over Distant Horizons terrain, volumetric clouds at Y 224 under the sky villages. Settings in `client/complementary-settings.txt`; in-game changes survive updates.
