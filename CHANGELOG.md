# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (in progress)

- Fixed: Productive Metalworks was still installed. A hand-placed jar in mod-files\mods isn't tracked by packwiz, so it was copied into the profile every update. download-mods.ps1 now deletes jars in mod-files\mods that packwiz doesn't track and that aren't in manual-mods.
- Fixed a hard crash (Java access violation) when a Tiered Foundry was on a Sable ship: it saved its interior as absolute positions, so after assembly the renderer drew millions of blocks of molten metal. Foundry interior and part links are now saved relative to the block (old saves recheck themselves), the renderer refuses impossible sizes, fluid quads write every vertex element, smoke appears where the ship is, and the foundry, research table and Navigation Console screens use vanilla's reach check so they stay open on ships.
- Tiered Foundry: every corner of a foundry, top to bottom, is now a wall block: vanilla Brick Wall, or the new Fire Brick Wall for fire bricks (6 from 6 fire bricks, or the stonecutter). Ponder updated. Fixed the casting basin's see-through side window.
- Night Raid Elites are named Apotheosis Elites (tools/raids/elites.py): Grave Warden (zombies), Bone Sentry (skeletons at Haven), Broodmother (spiders), Volatile Creeper, Hedge Witch, Illager Warlord, Ashen Knight (wither skeletons), Ravager Alpha. Apotheosis itself only has skeleton Elites from Frontier up, so most raiders were plain "Elite Zombie". Raids only; natural spawns unchanged. Every raid Elite then gets a random Apotheosis name (like Invaders), in the Elite's colour.
- Blood moons are now called Night Raids everywhere players see them (bars, chat, quests; command /silentraids nightraid start|stop). docs/ingame-text.txt (from tools/text/dump_text.py) lists all the pack's own player-facing text to proofread.
- Screens are vanilla width (176): the foundry controller and the Navigation Console were 200. Foundry controller: shows only its heaters' fuel (fuel slots or a full-height lava gauge), melting progress fills each slot from the bottom (blue while too cold), status on two lines. A foundry's floor is now one kind of heater. Foundries give light: heaters while burning, the controller and drains while there is metal, heat or something to melt.
- Tiered Foundry: Liquid Refractory is gone. Fire Bricks are now Fire Clay on a casting table glazed with 250 mB of Molten Glass (sand, glass or panes melted at 1000° in a foundry, so tier 1 can't be skipped); four craft a block. Molten glass also casts glass blocks in a basin.
- Tiered Foundry structure: the floor under the inside is now all heaters; a brick ring around the heaters holds the Air Intakes (renamed from Air Duct); every corner, top to bottom, must be the foundry's bricks. Fuel and lava use go up by one heater's worth per 9 heaters. Heat loss is slower with fire bricks (×0.6) and with each fan blowing in; the heat bar tooltip shows it. Ponder scenes for building a foundry and for the air intake.
- Research gates are off for now ("gates_off" in tools/questgen/stages.json): nothing is locked or hidden in EMI and the End is open. The stage seeds are kept; set it to false and rerun build_stages.py to bring them back.
- Fixed (Tiered Foundry): the molten surface inside the foundry showed a patch of the texture atlas; the lava gauge overflowed the screen with more than one lava heater (the client didn't know the tank's size).
- Fixed: joining a world crashed (Silent Gear's material loader can't read a switched-off file, and the retired Metalworks uru metal was one). Files removed by an update now arrive as EMP_TOMBSTONE files that the tools delete before building or installing, so the retired Metalworks overrides are really gone.
- Productive Metalworks and Silent Gear Metalworks removed: Tiered Foundry is now the pack's foundry. It casts Silent Gear parts (34 part casts, made by pouring gold over any part, or one-use sand casts; any metal that is a material for the part, the only way above iron), coats Silent Gear gear poured over on a table, casts gems (diamond), and the Obsidian Key fragment. New molten diamond (1450°), tyrian steel (1800°, alloyed with fire bricks until the pressure lid) and shulker. The Alloy Age foundry quests and the Alloy Age stage lock now use Tiered Foundry. The old heating coils, their recipes and the 2000° rule for diamond-tier metals are gone (fire bricks and lava now).
- Tiered Foundry: controller, drain and duct faces sit on the foundry's own bricks; items waiting to melt stand inside the foundry and molten metal fills it; smoke pours out while fans blow (more fans, more smoke; faster fans, faster smoke); the controller screen is vanilla style with a scrollbar; EMI melting pages show the heat source, temperature and a big output tank.
- New mod (in testing, beside Productive Metalworks): Tiered Foundry, tiers 1 to 2. A vanilla brick foundry with solid fuel heaters melts ores at ×1.2; a Create fan blowing into an air duct adds heat, alloying and ×1.4; fire bricks (cast from liquid refractory made in a tier 1 foundry) take it to 1900° with lava heaters and ×1.6. Items go in through the controller; drains and taps pour into casting tables (gold casts, one-use sand casts, fluid filter) and basins. Placeholder textures from Tinkers' Construct.
- Research editor: new Stage locks view to add, rename, reorder and delete stages and set what each locks (modded materials and everything crafted from them, tags, whole mods, never-lock exceptions, or dimensions). Saving rebuilds the lock files from every recipe in the jars and shows what each stage locks. The seeds moved from build_stages.py to tools/questgen/stages.json.
- Research Enchanting is now called Does Enchanting Overhaul (same mod id). Shelves and the chests research pulls from reach 3 blocks; the rune circle lies on the ground around the table; a focus in the table shows only its research tree (no close button); Echo's sequence lights blue so it can't be mistaken for your own presses.
- EXP Research: the Research Table screen is vanilla-container width and shorter, clear of EMI's search bar. New research editor (tools/researchedit, like the quest editor). All research and tabs cleared to be rebuilt in it: until new research grants the age stages, what they lock can't be made in survival.
- Research Focus books have the enchanted glint.

Breaking the table mid-way refunds the cost. Enchanting tables now show a turning rune circle and orbiting glyphs when you're near, and their glyph streams are far denser and wriggle along curving paths instead of straight lines (also from the chests research pulls from).
- The EXP Research screen is drawn from vanilla's own GUI art (panel, slots, advancement tabs and frames, buttons), so the grey, light and dark /uitheme looks apply to it; its tabs now sit above the window like the advancements screen.

The Create: New Age Retexture resource pack gives it Create-style textures. Wires, rods and electrum now come from Crafts & Additions.
