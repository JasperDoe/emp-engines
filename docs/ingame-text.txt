## Chapter: 5 · Exotic Age  -  Blaze and knowledge
  * Exotic Age  -  Learn this age at the Research Bench   [ex_root]
      This chapter guides you through the age once you've researched it.
      &eResearch unlocks:&r the Melee Elemental research focus (Fire Aspect, and Smite's lightning) when you bind books at an enchanting table. Exotic material chains come in a later update.
      &7Enchanting itself is open from the start: the quests below walk through it.
  * Echo   [ex_decipher]
      Put a plain book and some lapis in an enchanting table. Click a button to begin: the three buttons light up and chime in an order. Click them back in the same order.
      Three rounds, each one step longer. A wrong press costs 1 lapis and plays the round again. Books in chiseled bookshelves around the table play it slower.
      Finish the third round and the book becomes a &dResearch Focus&r for a random kind of gear.
  * First Research   [ex_research]
      Put the focus back in the table and open its research tree. Pick a level: it costs a few XP levels to start, then the table pulls its resource from a chest, barrel or vault up to 2 blocks away.
      A book holds one enchantment. Researching a different line replaces it, so bind more books for more enchantments.
      Feed it with a hopper or a Create belt and the bigger levels research themselves.
  * Chosen Enchantment   [ex_enchant]
      Shelve the researched focus in a chiseled bookshelf two blocks from the table, with a clear block between.
      Now put a tool, weapon or armour piece in the table: the buttons list what the shelved foci know. Pick exactly the enchantments you want. Books are never used up.

## Chapter: Night Raids & Gems  -  Every fifth night they come for you
  * The Night Raid  -  Read me   [bm_root]
      Every &c5th night&r raiders come for the Overworld: a &cNight Raid&r. You get a warning at dusk the evening before, and on the night &cbeds don't work&r.
      From nightfall, waves of raiders spawn &e30-40 blocks&r from every player who is on the ground. They come for you, and if they can't reach you they &cbreak in&r: about 30 seconds per block.
      They can only break plain Minecraft blocks. &aChests, furnaces, beds and every block from Create, Create: New Age, the foundry or any other mod are safe&r, and so is obsidian.
      Players &bon an airship or flying&r get no waves. Players close together share one siege.
      &7/silentraids status&r shows when the next one is.
  * World Tiers   [bm_tiers]
      Your &6world tier&r rises on its own with your play time and milestones:
      &aHaven&r at the start, &eFrontier&r at 5 h, &6Ascent&r at 15 h once you've been to the Nether, &cSummit&r at 30 h once you've killed the Wither, &5Pinnacle&r at 60 h once you've been to the End.
      A higher tier means harder night raids (more waves, better armour on the raiders, Elites and then Invaders) and better gems. It also lets your gear reach higher levels. Sieges get a little harder within a tier too, the longer you play.
  * Gear That Grows   [bm_levels]
      &6Silent Gear&r tools, weapons and armour gain &eXP&r as you use them: tools by mining (ores give more), weapons by hitting monsters, armour by taking hits from mobs.
      Every level gives &a+5% to all stats&r. Levels &b3, 6 and 9&r each add a &bgem socket&r.
      The top level depends on your world tier: 3 in Haven, 5 Frontier, 7 Ascent, 9 Summit, 10 Pinnacle. XP keeps counting at the cap.
      The Salvager won't take apart gear with levels or gems.
  * Your First Gem   [bm_gem]
      There's no enchanted 'affix' loot here: Apotheosis gives &dgems&r only.
      Monsters drop them now and then. Raid &6Elites&r always drop one, and &5Invaders&r several.
      Put a gem in a socket at the smithing table (gear in the middle, gem on the right). A &bSigil of Withdrawal&r takes gems back out.
  * Sigil of Socketing   [bm_sigil]
      A Sigil of Socketing adds &bone extra socket&r to any piece of gear, at the smithing table. Each item can take only one.
      Sigils can't be crafted. They come from quests, raid Elites and Invaders, and from beating every wave of a night raid.
  * Gem Cutting   [bm_cutting]
      Grind spare gems into &dgem dust&r at the Salvaging Table, then use the Gem Cutting Table to raise a gem's purity.
  * Summit  -  Kill the Wither   [bm_wither]
      Killing the Wither is the milestone for &cSummit&r (with 30 hours of play).
  * Pinnacle  -  Reach the End   [bm_end]
      Reaching the End is the milestone for &5Pinnacle&r (with 60 hours of play).
