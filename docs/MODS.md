# Custom mods

Three NeoForge 1.21.1 mods live in `mods/`, built together with Gradle:

| Mod | Folder | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| EXP Research | `mods/expresearch` | Research Table, blueprints, advancement-style research tree. Research costs XP points and sample items; a researched blueprint is right-clicked to unlock recipes (Chapters stages) for the whole FTB team. Hides locked items in EMI. |
| Chunk Upkeep | `mods/chunkupkeep` | Claim Ledger. Claims cost money from the player's Numismatics bank account; force-loaded chunks pay daily rent from the team owner's account. Normal FTB Chunks claiming is turned off (ops can bypass). Joining bonus and daily login bonus. |
| Create: Skybound | `mods/createskybound` | Vector Planar Computer, launch platform, Resonant Antennas and Resonance Pylons (3 tiers), the launch sequence, and timed runs into the Chaotic Skies. |

## Building

    .\tools\build-mods.ps1

This updates and downloads the pack's mods (the mods compile against those exact jars),
builds all three, and copies the jars into `pack\mods`. If it fails, send
`mods-errors.txt` or `build-mods.log`.

## Content that lives in the pack, not the mods

- Research tree: `pack/kubejs/data/skybound/expresearch/category/*.json` and `research/*.json`.
- Stage locks: `pack/kubejs/data/skybound/chapters/stages/*.json` (generated by `tools/questgen/build_stages.py`).

### Research node format

```json
{
  "category": "skybound:automation",
  "title": "Andesite Age",
  "description": "What it unlocks",
  "icon": "create:andesite_alloy",
  "x": 0, "y": 0,
  "parents": ["skybound:some_other_node"],
  "xp": 160,
  "samples": [{ "item": "minecraft:andesite", "count": 32 }],
  "stages": ["skybound:andesite_age"]
}
```

## Admin commands

    /expresearch grant <player> <research>
    /expresearch revoke <player> <research>
    /expresearch grantall <player>
    /expresearch list <player>

## Configs (server, per world: `serverconfig/`)

- `chunkupkeep-server.toml`: claim price, refund, rent, reach (48), ledgers per team (1), join and daily bonus.
- `createskybound-server.toml`: computer stress, charge needed (base + per ship mass), platform size, run length per pylon tier (30/50/70 minutes), warning time, exit height.

## Known gaps in this first version

- Launch visuals are particles and sounds; the custom beam renderer comes later.
- Every launch uses its own far-away region of one shared Chaotic Skies dimension, not a separate dimension.
- The team treasury is not in yet: claims are paid by the clicking player, rent by the team owner.
- Players who log out in the Chaotic Skies and come back after it collapsed are not yet killed.
- Ship lift and launch depend on Sable's physics and need tuning in game.
- FTB Chunks' own maximum-claims setting still applies; raise it in FTB Chunks' server config.
